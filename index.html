Questions Ask us ↗
What services does Fly Bodies Fitness offer?

Unique fitness experiences, including bungee fitness and Zumba — plus rehabilitation bungee and red light therapy.

Do I need prior experience to join a class?

No prior experience is necessary. Classes welcome every skill level, and instructors tailor each workout to you.

How do I sign up for a class?

Book through the website or visit the studio at 2038 Piney Green Road.

What should I wear to class?

Fitted workout clothes — avoid loose items that could catch in the equipment. Gym shoes or sneakers are recommended.

Are classes available for private groups or events?

Yes. Private group sessions and event bookings are customized for celebrations and team activities.

Is there an age limit for classes?

Most classes are designed for ages 16 and older, with specialized options available on request.

Do you offer memberships or packages?

Yes — membership options and class packages to fit different schedules and budgets.

What safety measures are in place?

High-quality, well-maintained equipment, and every instructor is certified and trained.

How can I contact Fly Bodies Fitness?

Call (910) 240-2968, message us on Facebook or Instagram, or visit in person.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellation for bungee fitness parties is $50. No shows — no refunds.
